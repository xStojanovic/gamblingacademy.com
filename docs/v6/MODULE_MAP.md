# V6 Module Map

## Public
`modules/public` — acquisition website and public presentation components.

## Learning
`modules/learning` — course catalogue, learning paths and course persistence services.

## Authoring
`modules/authoring` — modular lesson/content block authoring.

## Learner
`modules/learner` — learner progress, notes, resources, transcript and dashboard state.

## Company
`modules/company` — customer administration, employees, teams, private academy and provisioning.

## Admin
`modules/admin` — OpsAcademy owner controls and operating workspace.

## Assessments
Owner assessment studio plus learner delivery APIs/components.

## Competencies
`modules/competencies` — skills, role profiles and readiness.

## Onboarding
`modules/onboarding` — 30/60/90-day onboarding programs.

## Cohorts — V6
`modules/cohorts` — cohort programs, enrollment capacity, live sessions and learner cohort experience.

## White-label — V6
`modules/whitelabel` — customer academy identity, custom-domain and theme controls.

## Sales — V6
`modules/sales` — B2B opportunity/pilot/proposal pipeline.

## Interoperability — V6
`modules/interoperability` — SCORM/xAPI/LTI enterprise controls and package architecture.

## Quality — V6
`modules/quality` — course freshness/accessibility/review quality controls.

## Reporting
`modules/reporting` — company reporting and V6 recurring report schedules.

## Credentials
`modules/credentials` — credential templates, lifecycle and verification.

## Billing
`modules/billing` — plan/subscription and Stripe integration boundaries.

## Auth & Permissions
`modules/auth`, `modules/permissions` — production role resolution, middleware and RBAC.

## Infrastructure
`modules/infrastructure` — environment and Supabase REST boundaries.

## Integrations
`modules/integrations` — external system adapters.

## AI
`modules/ai` — grounded tutor architecture.

## Shared
`modules/shared` — application shell, theme, navigation and shared design components.
